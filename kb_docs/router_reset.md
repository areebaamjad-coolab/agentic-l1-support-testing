## Quick steps
- Unplug the router for 30 seconds, then plug it back in.
- Wait 2-3 minutes for it to reboot fully.

## Factory reset (last resort)
- Press and hold the RESET pinhole button for 10-15 seconds.
- Reconfigure Wi-Fi name/password and ISP/PPPoE settings if required.
